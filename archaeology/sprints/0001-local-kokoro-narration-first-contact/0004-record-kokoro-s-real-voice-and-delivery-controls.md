# Record Kokoro's real voice and delivery controls

## Objective

Find out what Kokoro actually gives us for delivery control, so the presentation work can plan
against the real surface instead of a hoped-for one.

Record the answer. Do not build on it yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- Voice inventory, speaking-rate control, and any prosody affordances are established by reading
  the runtime's actual inputs, not its marketing.
- Absent capabilities are named explicitly, since decision:6 assumed provider-supplied delivery
  instructions would exist.
- Anything that materially constrains later design is recorded as a dragon rather than left in a
  commit message.
- No cuecraft-specific speech markup is introduced.
