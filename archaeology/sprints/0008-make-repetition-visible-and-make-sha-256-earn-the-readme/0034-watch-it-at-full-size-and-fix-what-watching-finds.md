# Watch it at full size and fix what watching finds

## Objective

Watch it, at full size, at every moment that matters, and fix what watching finds.

The field is the part that cannot be judged from a test. Specific predictions to check, because a
prediction that goes unchecked is a hope:

- **Density.** Sixty-four cells inside a chamber inside a chamber. Are they discrete, or texture?
- **Order.** Can a viewer see which way it fills without being told?
- **The count.** Is the running number unmistakable, or does it fight the field?
- **The moment.** Coming out of one round and finding sixty-four — does that land as *the same
  thing, many times*, or as a new picture that happens to be next door?
- **Compression.** A dense field of small high-contrast cells is the worst case for h264. Check
  the upload-sized projection, not just the master.
- **Title clearance.** dragon:14 says a world more than one plate tall grazes the heading. A field
  is not a world, but it is tall.

## Acceptance criteria

- Frames inspected at 1920x1080 for: outer map, entry to compression, sixteen words before
  expansion, expansion at a quarter, half, three quarters, complete, entry to one round, T1/T2 and
  both injections, the reveal of sixty-four, the field at half, the field complete, feed-forward,
  return to the outer algorithm, the digest.
- Every transition asked: does the viewer know where they are, and does the picture say what the
  voice claims?
- Defects fixed in the artifact first, and in the machinery only when the artifact cannot carry it.
- The three existing showpieces re-render identically.
- The compressed upload copy is checked and still excellent.
