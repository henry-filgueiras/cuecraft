# Watch them, and let the frames choose

## Objective

Choose a variant from the frames, and say why in terms of what the frames show rather than what the
CSS intends.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every variant is looked at on both specimens.
- The decision names which perceptual property each variant did and did not deliver, including for
  the ones rejected.
- If the baseline wins, the round says so and the implementation is reverted.
