# Three specimens, watched, and what they showed

## Objective

Three specimens, rendered through the ordinary pipeline and actually watched, and an honest account
of what they showed.

## Acceptance criteria

- `examples/` gains one small focused subtitle example, written as the other examples are: a file
  that explains its own point in comments and demonstrates exactly one thing.
- `examples/aside.yaml` renders with subtitles — the important specimen, because it is the one with
  two narrators, and because the next film is a two-narrator explainer.
- One ordinary single-narrator film renders with subtitles enabled and is compared against its own
  current render.
- Frames inspected during development, not only at the end, across at least: a statement slide, a
  list, a specimen, a world under camera movement, and a protocol.
- Written answers to every perceptual question the sprint asks, including the ones whose honest
  answer is "worse".
- No generated MP4 is committed (decision:3).
