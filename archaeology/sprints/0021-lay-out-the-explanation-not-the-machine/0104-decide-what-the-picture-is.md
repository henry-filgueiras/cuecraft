# Decide what the picture is

## Objective

Record, as a decision, what the picture is — before the layout code that assumes an answer.

Two things have to be written down and neither is a formality. First: the picture stops being an
atlas of a machine and becomes an explanation of a telling, so the same machine narrated two ways
draws two ways. Second: decision:52's formal invariant permits seeding and its prose forbids it,
and the round has to say which half moves and which stands rather than quietly exploiting the gap.

## Acceptance criteria

- A decision artifact stating the atlas/explanation choice, what it costs a viewer, and what it
  buys, with the measurements from sprint:21's rationale behind it rather than the argument alone.
- It says precisely which sentence of decision:52 is superseded and which sentences still hold, and
  decision:52 is marked as superseded in the part that moved.
- It states what remains refused: nothing is hidden, dropped, shrunk or unlabelled; every declared
  transition is on the map, routed and captioned at full size; the core is derived and never
  authorable; the scenario's order still moves nothing.
- It states the failure mode it is accepting — that a viewer cannot learn the machine's shape
  independently of the telling — and why that is the right trade for a narrated presentation.

## What was recorded instead

decision:53, and it does not make the choice this task was written to make.

task:103 established that the seeded layout cannot be drawn with the dependency this repository
has. So the atlas-versus-explanation question never came due: there was no implementation to put
behind the explanation, and choosing it in the abstract would have been inventing a decision the
round did not have to make. decision:53 records the question as live and unanswered, states
decision:52's invariant/prose gap precisely, and says that the prose is what a later round has to
re-argue if it finds a way.

The rest of the criteria are met — what remains refused is stated, and the measurements are behind
it rather than the argument alone. The first criterion is met in the negative, on purpose.
