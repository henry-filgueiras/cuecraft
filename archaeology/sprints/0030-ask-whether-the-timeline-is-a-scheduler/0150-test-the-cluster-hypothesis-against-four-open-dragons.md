# Test the cluster hypothesis against four open dragons

## Objective

Test the cluster hypothesis: that dragon:12, dragon:15, dragon:24 and dragon:27 are four symptoms of
one missing ownership model. Take each in turn and say whether an explicit cooperative scheduler
would close it, weaken it, or leave it exactly where it is.

This task is where the round is most likely to disappoint itself, which is why it exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- A verdict per dragon, each with the reason stated in terms of what the scheduler would and would
  not decide.
- Where a dragon is *not* closed, the actual missing thing is named — so the round leaves the
  dragons better characterised than it found them even if the hypothesis fails.
- The result is recorded in the sprint or in a verdict artifact, not only in a conversation.
