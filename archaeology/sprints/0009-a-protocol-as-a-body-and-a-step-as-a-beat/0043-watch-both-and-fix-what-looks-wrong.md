# Watch both, and fix what looks wrong

## Objective

Watch both films and fix what is wrong with them. This is the task the sprint is actually about;
every task before it is machinery in service of it.

The rule from decision:23 and the whole of the archaeology applies: a bad frame is a renderer
defect. The repair is a better default, and if a default cannot be found the finding is recorded
rather than papered over with a key.

## Acceptance criteria

- Both films rendered end to end and watched, not sampled.
- A written critique of each against comprehensibility, hierarchy, typography, spacing, motion,
  camera, pacing, synchronisation, clutter, and whether attention lands where the explanation
  expects it.
- Every obvious defect either fixed or recorded as a dragon with the measurement that found it.
- The rendered artifacts and their sources preserved so that Henry and ChatGPT can review them
  independently.
