# Quote a block that fits, and say less over a frozen frame

## Objective

Make slide 4's specimen fit the box it was given, by quoting a block that fits rather than by
changing what a composition does when it does not.

The quoted region is slide 3's `say:` block, and `opens:` shows a construct whole — the line plus
everything indented beneath it — so there is no way to show part of one. The block has to get
shorter, which means the sentences the film is narrated with have to get shorter.

That is a better deck anyway. A sentence spoken over a frozen frame should be the length of a held
breath, and three of the current ones are two clauses long because they were written to be read
rather than to stop a film.

## Acceptance criteria

- Slide 3's `say:` block is short enough that slide 4's specimen fits inside its box with the
  subtitle band present. Verified on a frame extracted at full resolution, not inferred from a
  line count.
- All four named states are still reached, `replay:` still runs, and the three marks the specimen
  calls out — `play:`, a `during:` sentence and `replay:` — are all visible in the quote.
- Nothing spoken becomes untrue in the shortening. The fifteen centimetres, the twelve seconds and
  the twelve kilometres an hour are measured values and either survive intact or come out.
- The slide gets shorter, not longer.
