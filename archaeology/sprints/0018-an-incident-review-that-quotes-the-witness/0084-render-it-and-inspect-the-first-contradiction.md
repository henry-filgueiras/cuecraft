# Render it, and inspect the first contradiction

## Objective

Render the deck to MP4 and inspect the first recall — slide 4 quoting slide 2 — at full size,
frame by frame across its boundaries.

## Acceptance criteria

- A complete MP4 rendered from the compiled deck, with its duration reported.
- Frames captured and looked at, at 1920x1080, for: the frame before entry, the first quotation
  frame, a middle frame carrying the historical subtitle and an advanced local clock, the final
  quotation frame, the returned frame, the subtitle-free pause, and the first frame of the reply.
- A recorded judgement on whether the contradiction is legible without knowing the feature exists,
  and whether the replay reads as evidence rather than as navigation.
