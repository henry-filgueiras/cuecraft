# Render, look, and correct

## Objective

Render both films through the real pipeline and let the frames change the result. First pass.

## Acceptance criteria

- Both render to completion through `cuecraft render`; the exact commands and paths recorded.
- Evidence extracted covering: initial topology and inferred start, first transition, a silent
  arrival during its hold, a camera hold, a camera move, a revisit, a repeated transition, a
  parallel transition identity in leases, the heartbeat self-loop, ledger overflow, cumulative
  counts, the final occupancy with the full ledger, and the final canonical overview.
- Before/after contact sheets where practical.
- Every frame checked for clipping, label collision, ambiguous arrows, unreadable type, ledger and
  graph competing, lost occupancy, history drowning topology, misleading terminal styling, camera
  thrash, gratuitous near-overview shots, insufficient dwell, excessive final silence, counts
  obscuring labels, and leases remaining unintelligible at the overview.
- Defects found are recorded with what was wrong, and fixed or explicitly deferred to a dragon.
