# Choose the mechanism, and say what the other one would have bought

## Objective

Choose, and record what the losing mechanism would have bought.

## Acceptance criteria

- A decision artifact naming the mechanism, with the spike evidence attached rather than referenced
  vaguely.
- An explicit account of what was given up. If regions win, that includes resolution independence,
  in-artifact metadata, and the animation surface — and dragon:31 stays open.
- dragon:32 either closed by the decision or updated with what was learned.
- If neither mechanism is worth its price, that is the decision, and the round stops here.

## Chosen: regions over the protocol — decision:57

The spike made the choice easy by making it not a choice. **Cairo's SVG has no ids, no grouping and
no `<text>`**, so it cannot say which panel is Asia Pacific either. Addressing needs the program to
declare where it drew things whichever format carries the picture, and once that is true, PNG is the
format already shipping and SVG is a second question.

## What SVG would have bought, explicitly

Not addressing — that was the surprise, and it is why this round could pick the cheaper option
without giving up the thing it was for. What it would have bought:

- **Resolution independence.** A `viewBox` scales to any room without redrawing, which dissolves most
  of dragon:31 rather than managing it. Regions do nothing for that, and dragon:31 stays open.
- **In-artifact styling.** Inlined into the DOM, cuecraft's stylesheet reaches every element in the
  picture. A one-line rule matching the accent fill recoloured all sixteen bars. That is a *category*
  handle, never an instance one — but it exists because decision:55 handed R the palette, and nobody
  designed it.
- **Immunity to dragon:4.** Text is outlined into paths, so an SVG exhibit depends on no host font.
  Nothing else in this repository can say that.
- **An animation surface.** Emphasis could be a style on a mark rather than a veil drawn over a
  photograph.

Parked as idea:24 with those four reasons attached, so the next round that wants any of them does not
have to rediscover that the fifth reason — addressing — was never on offer.

## What was given up by choosing regions

Emphasis is subtractive: a veil over the picture with a hole in it, because a raster cannot show part
of itself at a different opacity. Granularity is whatever the program is willing to name and nothing
finer. And `shows:` is a third key on `exhibit:`, forced by anchors resolving at parse time while R
runs afterwards — held honest by a mismatch check in both directions rather than by trust.

dragon:32 is closed by decision:57 for what a program will name, and the residue — reaching something
the program did not think to declare — is written into the decision rather than left implied.
